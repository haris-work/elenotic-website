Why Pakistan, first

Pakistan is an environment where the distributed-energy problem is especially visible: rapid rooftop solar adoption, expensive electricity, grid reliability challenges, growing battery use, widespread backup generation and fragmented household setups.

Elenotic is not a Pakistan-only solar company. These conditions help us study the problem closely.

A global problem

Distributed solar, batteries, EVs, flexible loads, buildings, microgrids and increasingly dynamic electricity markets are reshaping energy systems everywhere. We believe the missing piece is coordination.

Other companies are working on solar optimization, home energy management and distributed-energy management. Our thesis is a hardware-agnostic, system-level, learning-based layer built around distributed energy from the start.
