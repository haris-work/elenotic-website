A connected energy system

Elenotic is an early-stage energy technology company exploring the intelligence layer for distributed energy. This site describes our thesis and development direction.

THE CHALLENGE

Distributed energy is growing. The intelligence coordinating it is still fragmented.

A single home may combine solar, a battery, grid power, a generator, an EV, air conditioners, pumps, water heaters and other appliances. They often come from different manufacturers, with different data and different controls.

How do you decide where every unit of available energy should go, now and in the future?

Today that decision often falls to the homeowner: when to use solar, when to charge or discharge the battery, when to rely on the grid, when to preserve backup power. The best answer can change every few minutes, and depends on tomorrow's weather, expected demand, tariffs and what the household values.

OUR APPROACH

One intelligence layer. A more connected energy system.

Hardware-agnostic

An intelligence layer designed to coordinate assets across vendors, rather than a single-vendor ecosystem.

System-level

Decisions across generation, storage, consumption, backup and flexible loads, not just monitoring.

Learning

Forecasting, optimization, machine learning and reinforcement learning, rather than static rules alone.
