Technology

Elenotic is building an intelligence layer that enables distributed energy systems to understand, coordinate and optimize generation, storage and consumption.

Everything on this page describes our thesis and development direction.

The intelligence loop

We frame the system as a continuous loop.

  1. ObserveGather data from solar systems, batteries, the grid, appliances, EVs, meters, weather and tariffs.
  2. UnderstandMaintain a current picture of generation, consumption, battery state, grid status, appliance state and constraints.
  3. PredictUse machine learning to estimate solar generation, household demand, future energy availability and battery behavior.
  4. OptimizeDecide how energy should be allocated, balancing cost, solar use, battery health, backup needs, appliance constraints, preferences and comfort.
  5. ActOver time, coordinate controllable assets.
  6. LearnUse real outcomes and feedback to improve predictions and decisions. Then the loop repeats.

Prediction and optimization are different jobs

We intend to use Neural Networks to recognize patterns and estimate what is likely to happen: solar output from history, weather and system data; household demand from consumption history and routines; battery behavior from past operation.

Prediction asks

What is likely to happen?

Optimization asks

What should we do about it?

If tomorrow is forecast to be cloudy, the system still has to decide what to do today. That means weighing competing goals: lower cost, higher renewable utilization, less waste, battery health, backup capacity and comfort.

Reinforcement learning, as a research direction

Reinforcement learning lets an agent learn sequential decisions by interacting with an environment: it observes the energy state, takes an action, sees the outcome and receives a reward. Objectives could include lower cost, better solar utilization, backup reliability, less unnecessary battery cycling and maintained comfort.
